# Hẻm 47 – Project Direction

Hẻm 47: The Quiet World is an independent Vietnamese narrative game focused on raising awareness of online scams, human trafficking, and scam compounds across Southeast Asia.

Project founder: IngIngmachi

Project responsibilities:
- Original concept and product vision
- Narrative, story development and character design
- Game design and gameplay direction
- UX/UI direction and creative decisions
- AI-assisted development coordination
- Product testing and release planning

The project uses AI-assisted workflows, including Claude, for software development and implementation.
